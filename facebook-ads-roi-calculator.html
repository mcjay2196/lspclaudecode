Why build systems & organic before you scale ads

Ads only multiply what already works. Pour traffic into a business with no website, no lead capture and no follow-up, and most of that spend leaks straight out the bottom. Fix the funnel first — then ads become an accelerator instead of a gamble.

1

Build the systems

Before a dollar goes to ads, put the plumbing in place:

  • A website with a real lead-capture form
  • Name, phone, email & suburb on every enquiry
  • Instant auto-reply + fast human follow-up
  • A CRM that stores every lead forever
  • 1, 2 & 6-month seasonal follow-up automations
2

Grow organically

Earn traffic you don't pay for every click on:

  • Google Business Profile & local reviews
  • Local SEO so you rank in your suburbs
  • Referrals & word of mouth from happy clients
  • A backlog of warm visitors who know your name
3

Then run ads to retarget

Now ads work with a warm audience, not cold strangers:

  • Retarget people who already found you organically
  • Every click lands on a page that captures the lead
  • Follow-up automations keep leads warm for months
  • Recurring revenue compounds instead of resetting

⚠ Where the Messenger-only approach leaks money

Leads go cold. You reply between jobs or at day's end — the hottest leads have already booked someone else.
The 24-hour window closes. Facebook stops you messaging back after a day, so cold leads are gone for good.
No contact details. No name, email, phone or suburb means no way to ever reach them again.
No follow-up. No 1, 2 or 6-month seasonal offers — every lead is a one-shot.
Data trapped in Messenger. Your customer list lives in a chat inbox you don't own or control.
No recurring engine. One-time jobs never get nudged into regular fortnightly runs.
How the numbers work: Impressions = spend ÷ CPM × 1,000. Clicks = impressions × CTR. On the website path, leads = clicks × landing-page rate; jobs = leads × booking rate, plus reactivated cold leads via follow-up; recurring clients = jobs × recurring rate. Recurring revenue (ARR) = new recurring clients × services per year × recurring value. Lifetime value (cohort) = one-time job revenue + (recurring clients × services/year × recurring value × lifespan). ROAS = 12-month revenue ÷ annual spend. All figures are estimates for illustration and planning only — actual results depend on your market, offer, creative and follow-through. Currency in AUD.
